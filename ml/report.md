# Phase 5 model report

Generated by `ml/train.py`. ETA and acceptance: first 70% of history trains, last 30% tests. Demand: 5-fold blocked cross-validation over time.

## ETA: seconds from accept to pickup
77,747 trips to train, 33,321 to test.

| | MAE | median abs error | mean bias |
|---|---|---|---|
| formula: distance × 1.3 ÷ 9.0 m/s | 19.8 s | 12.1 s | +19.8 s |
| linear model (Huber) | 4.2 s | 2.4 s | -0.4 s |
| gradient boosting | 5.6 s | 2.6 s | +3.3 s |

Learned: -1.59 s + 88.7 s per km (≈ 11.3 m/s effective, vs the formula's 6.9).

## Acceptance: will this driver say yes?
116,297 offers to train, 49,842 to test. 66.2% of training offers were accepted.

| | log loss | Brier | AUC | mean predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| smoothed history (the Phase 3 `p`) | 0.6452 | 0.2259 | 0.537 | 69.0% |
| logistic regression | 0.6336 | 0.2209 | 0.538 | 66.4% |
| gradient boosting | 0.6341 | 0.2211 | 0.533 | 66.3% |

Actual acceptance in the test period: 66.7%. Lower log loss / Brier is better; AUC is how well it ranks yes-drivers above no-drivers (0.5 = coin flip).

Standardised weights: logit_p +0.159, distance_km +0.000, attempt -0.004, urgent +0.001

## Demand: bookings per zone in the next 5 minutes
53,456 simulator bookings; 5-fold blocked cross-validation over 69 five-minute windows × 624 active zones (each window predicted by shares learned without its block).

| | MAE per zone-window | MAE on the 20 busiest zones |
|---|---|---|
| each zone's own last 5 min (current) | 1.532 | 6.695 |
| zone share × city-wide last 5 min (model) | 1.191 | 5.207 |

The model pools the whole city's recent volume (lots of data, little noise) and splits it by each zone's long-run share, instead of trusting one zone's last few bookings.
